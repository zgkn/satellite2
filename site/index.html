Fire map Sumatra & Kalimantan

Flagged areas

Severe areas have fires packed tight and a lot of heat; watch areas have one of the two. Hotspots are only the pointer: NOAA VIIRS 375 m active fire (EFIRE; NOAA-20, NOAA-21, S-NPP). The product is the latest Himawari-9 AHI image (JMA, via the NOAA Open Data Dissemination program) cropped to each cluster. Day Fire RGB follows the NOAA/CIRA recipe on AHI bands; at night the 3.9 µm band is shown.

Streamlines: ECMWF IFS open data, 925 hPa wind, licensed CC-BY-4.0. They are model output, not observations, and can disagree with what the clouds show.

Basemaps: Esri World Imagery, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Coastlines: Natural Earth (public domain). Himawari 3.9 µm pixels are 2 km, so images look blocky. Not an official fire product.
